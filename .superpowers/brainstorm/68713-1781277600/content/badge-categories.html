What kinds of badges should exist?

Select all that apply — badges can come from multiple categories.

A

Milestone badges

Earned by hitting activity counts. Automatic, no community validation needed.

📝 First Post 💬 100 Posts 🃏 First Deck Shared 📦 500 Cards Added 🗓️ 1-Year Member
B

Quality badges

Earned when the community validates your contributions. Harder to get, more prestigious.

⭐ First Upvote 🔥 Hot Take (50 upvotes) ✅ Best Answer 💎 Deck of the Week
C

Admin-awarded badges

Custom badges admins can create and manually grant. Good for special events, tournaments, or recognition.

🏆 Tournament Winner 🤝 Community Helper ✨ Staff Pick
