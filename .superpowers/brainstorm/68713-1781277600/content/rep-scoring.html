How should reputation be earned?

The current User.reputation field (0–5 rating) would be replaced with a point-accumulation system.

A

Activity only

Points for actions you take — posting, replying, sharing a deck, adding cards. Community validation not required.

Pros

  • Easy to earn, good for new users
  • Simple to implement

Cons

  • Doesn't reward quality — spam farms rep
  • High posters ≠ valuable contributors
B

Upvotes only

Points purely from community upvotes on your threads and posts. Quality-first.

Pros

  • Rep = community trust, not just activity
  • Discourages low-effort posts

Cons

  • Slow for new users with no history
  • Lurkers can't earn without posting first
C

Both — activity + upvotes (Recommended)

Small points for activity, larger points for upvotes received. Gives new users a path in while still rewarding quality.

Example point values:
+1 · Post a reply  |  +2 · Create a thread  |  +3 · Share a deck
+5 · Receive an upvote on a post  |  +10 · Receive an upvote on a thread
+15 · Best answer selected (Q&A format)

Pros

  • Balanced — newcomers can participate
  • Quality still matters most
  • Ties into Q&A feature naturally

Cons

  • More moving parts to implement
