What happens when content gets flagged?

A user taps "Flag" on a forum post or chat message. What should the system do next?

A

Always manual review

Flag goes to the mod queue. Content stays visible until a moderator hides or removes it. Nothing automatic.

Pros

  • No false positives from coordinated flag brigading
  • Mods stay in control

Cons

  • Harmful content stays live until a mod checks the queue
B

Auto-hide after threshold

After 3 unique flags from different users, content is automatically hidden pending review. Mods can restore or remove it.

Pros

  • Bad content disappears fast without mod intervention
  • Community self-polices to a degree

Cons

  • Coordinated users could abuse the threshold
C

Auto-hide + notify admin

Same as B, but reaching the threshold also sends an in-app notification to all admins/editors so the queue gets reviewed quickly.

Pros

  • Fast response loop
  • Mods always know when something needs attention

Cons

  • Notification spam if flagging is abused
