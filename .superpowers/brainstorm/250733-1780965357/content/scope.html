Video Room — What's the scope?

Your existing life counter is already full-featured. How deeply should the video room connect to it?

A

Lightweight

Video grid + simple shared life totals only. Players join a link, see each other on camera, click +/- on their life. Nothing else synced.

Build time: ~2 days  ·  Existing life counter stays separate

B

Integrated

Video grid embedded inside the life counter view. Everyone's life totals, commander damage, and turn order sync in real time across all screens.

Build time: ~4-5 days  ·  Extends the existing life counter

C

Full Game Room

Dedicated room: video + synced life counter + each player picks their deck before the game. Most like SpellTable. Records result to game log automatically.

Build time: ~1 week  ·  New view, ties into playgroups + deck builder
