Mobile Admin Panel — Navigation Approach

Right now on mobile, the sidebar accordion (4 groups × 4-6 tabs each) squeezes into a small 256px-tall scrollable box above the content, and can bleed past the modal edge. Which navigation pattern should replace it?

A

Drill-down (Recommended)

Tap a group → full-screen list of its tabs → tap a tab → full-screen tab content with a "← Back" button. Only one screen visible at a time, no nested scrolling, no sidebar+content fighting for width.

B

Slide-out drawer

A "Menu" button opens a full-height drawer (like the app's own main sidebar) listing all groups/tabs. Tapping a tab closes the drawer and shows that tab full-width. Familiar pattern, but adds an extra open/close step every time you switch tabs.

C

Horizontal scrolling tab bar

Sidebar becomes a horizontally-scrollable pill bar pinned under the header (group selector dropdown, then tab chips for that group). Content is always visible below it — no full-screen navigation step, but ~16-24 tabs as scrollable chips can be fiddly to browse on a phone.
