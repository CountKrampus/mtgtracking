What's the primary purpose of the forum?

The app already has global chat, per-card/deck comments, and direct messages. The forum adds organized, persistent discussion.

A

Deck Help & Strategy

Players post decks for feedback, discuss archetypes, share brews, and talk competitive vs casual metas

B

Community Hub

General MTG discussion, local event organizing, playgroup coordination, news & lore

C

Trading Post

Buy / sell / trade cards, price checks, have lists, want lists, and deal negotiation

D

All of the Above

Separate categories for each — deck help, community, trading, and anything else that comes up
