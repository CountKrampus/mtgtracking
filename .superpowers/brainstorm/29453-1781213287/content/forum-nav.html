Where does the forum live in the app?

The sidebar already has Navigation, Gameplay, Social, and other groups. Pick the approach that fits best.

A

Sidebar entry — own full page

A "Forum" link in the sidebar (under Community or its own group). Clicking it replaces the main view with the full forum — categories, threads, and posts.

B

Tab inside Playgroups

The forum lives inside a playgroup — each playgroup gets its own forum. More focused but scoped to your group only, not the whole community.

C

Both — global + per-playgroup

A global community forum in the sidebar AND a forum tab inside each playgroup. Global for the whole community; playgroup forum for your local crew.
