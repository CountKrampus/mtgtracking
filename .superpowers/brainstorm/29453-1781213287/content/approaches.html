3 Approaches to the Forum

Same features, different navigation models. Pick the one that feels right.

A

Classic Forum (Recommended)

Categories → Thread list → Thread view. Familiar to anyone who's used MTGSalvation or Commander forums. Clear hierarchy, easy to navigate.

Pros

  • Familiar to MTG players
  • Clear organization
  • Easy to find specific topics

Cons

  • 3 clicks to read a post
  • Active threads can get buried
B

Tagged Feed (Reddit-style)

Single scrollable feed of all posts. Filter by tag (Deck Help, Trading, etc.). Sorted by hot/new/top. No categories page — just the feed.

Pros

  • Fast — content immediately visible
  • Active posts always surface
  • Modern, familiar feel

Cons

  • Can feel chaotic without moderation
  • Tags need discipline to work well
C

Hybrid (Feed + Categories)

Homepage is a "recent/hot" feed. Left sidebar shows categories to filter. Best of both: discovery up front, structure available when needed.

Pros

  • Newcomers see activity immediately
  • Power users use category nav
  • Most flexible long-term

Cons

  • Most complex to build
  • Two navigation patterns to maintain
